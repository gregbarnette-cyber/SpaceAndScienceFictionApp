# this set (plus the science subset of requirements.txt) into a micromamba/conda env
# instead of pip, then point query.py at it with SPACE_APP_DUST_PYTHON — the dust
# subcommands re-dispatch to that interpreter; it stays a no-op on WSL/Linux/macOS. Full
