Solvent Habitable Zone Calculator P4

Generalizes the hardcoded Alternate-HZ divisor table into a first-class engine: the orbital band where a chosen solvent stays liquid, from its liquid range and the star's luminosity. At albedo 0.3 a named solvent's band edges land on its 1-atm boiling (inner) / freezing (outer) points.

Plan details
  • compute_solvent_zone(luminosity_solar, solvent=None, t_low_k=None, t_high_k=None, albedo=0.3)
  • M1 surface model: T_ref = 314.9·(1−A)0.25 (= 288 K at A=0.3); S_eff = (T_edge/T_ref)⁴; AU = √(L / S_eff)
  • Reproduces the legacy bands at A=0.3 (water 373/273 K → divisors 2.8/0.8) with the corrected (1−A)0.25 albedo law. Hydrogen uses 20.3/13.8 K (not the legacy 64 K). CO₂ is pressure-conditional (≥ 5.2 atm).
  • Self-validating (Phase H contract): L>0, 0≤A<1, 0<t_low<t_high, known solvent → else {"error"}.

Ice-Line Calculator P5

Volatile condensation fronts on the M2 equilibrium model (no greenhouse) — the single canonical water snow line (170 K / 2.68 AU) plus CO₂ / NH₃ / N₂ / CO fronts. Disk fronts (CO, N₂) are set by disk-midplane temperature, so their irradiation placement is illustrative (flagged below).

Plan details
  • compute_ice_lines(luminosity_solar, albedo=0.0) → {lines:[{species, t_cond_k, au, lm, kind, disk_line, note}]}
  • M2: T_ref = 278.5·(1−A)0.25; AU = √L · (T_ref / T_cond)². Default albedo 0 (ice grains).
  • Single water snow line (170 K → 2.68 AU) — no dual line: present-day & formation both ≈ 2.7 AU for the Sun.
  • The √L irradiation law does not cleanly place disk-midplane lines (CO/N₂) — surfaced as a caveat.
Disk-front rows (CO, N₂) are flagged — their true location is set by disk-midplane temperature, not stellar irradiation. Complementary to these, the regions table's lh2Line (~62 K, 20 AU, M2) is the 1-atm N₂/CO surface-frost reading — a different convention (P1b); both are shown and both are correct for what they represent.

Alternative Solvents — Reference Table P6

Static reference (à la Main Sequence Star Properties). Liquid ranges, the implied equilibrium-T band at A = 0.3, and the Bains 2024 plausibility verdict. No computation — the same built-in table the Solvent HZ & Ice-Line calculators use.

Plan details
  • Source: module constant _SOLVENTS in core/equations.py (single source of truth).
  • Bains/Petkowski/Seager 2024 four-criterion verdict stored per entry as plausibility.
Liquid-Range Bars (Temperature, K — log axis)
Bains 2024 headline: only water and concentrated sulfuric acid are rated both functional and plausibly abundant on rocky worlds; ammonia fails the occurrence criterion; CO₂ is the standout non-protonating solvent (but needs ≥ 5.2 atm to have a liquid phase at all).

Alternate Habitable Zone Regions P1 · P2 · P7

The existing Star-System-Regions output, corrected & extended (Package A). Solvent bands use M1 (surface); snow/ice lines use M2 (equilibrium). Sound bands (water / ammonia / methane) are unchanged.

Plan details

  • P1c: snowLine → 0.139 (170 K, 2.68 AU; M2). Value fix (was 0.04 / 5 AU — surface model misapplied to an ice line).
  • P1b: lh2Line value is correct under M2 (62 K, 20 AU) — relabel only. P1d: fluorosilicone → hypothetical high-T (relabel).
  • P1e: planetaryTemperature albedo bug (1−A)¹ → (1−A)0.25 (separate fix; not shown in this table).
  • P2: new CO₂ / sulfur / water-ammonia / sulfuric-acid solvent bands (M1).
  • P7: per-model implied-T column + citations + the M1/M2 disclaimer.
In the app the Status pills are: sound (unchanged) · new · relabeled · corrected value. Solvent-band temps shown at the regions default A=0.3 (M1); snow/ice temps at A=0 (M2). The Solvent-HZ & Ice-Line calculators expose albedo.
P7c disclaimer: the M1 solvent bands are surface temperatures with Earth-like greenhouse (A=0.3) — they run closer-in than greenhouse-corrected HZs (water 0.60–1.12 AU vs Kopparapu's ~0.95–1.37 AU). The M2 snow/ice lines are bare equilibrium (no greenhouse), correct for ice condensation in vacuum.
