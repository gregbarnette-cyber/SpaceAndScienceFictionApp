O3 · Mass–Radius Diagram

Log–log scatter of system planets against constant-density composition curves and the eight Solar-System reference points.

Plan details (from future_phases.md)
  • prepare_mass_radius(planets, mass_key, radius_key, name_key) — generic over NASA (pl_bmasse/pl_rade) & HWC (P_MASS/P_RADIUS); filters to planets with both values.
  • Curves: R = (M/(ρ/ρ⊕))^(1/3), ρ⊕=5.51 — iron 7.9, rock 5.51, water 1.0, Jupiter 1.33. Deliberately constant-density, not Zeng models.
  • Tab added only when ≥ 1 planet has both mass & radius.
